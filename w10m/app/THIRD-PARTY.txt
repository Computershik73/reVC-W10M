OpenAL32.dll
    OpenAL Soft 1.24.3 (https://github.com/kcat/openal-soft), GNU Library General Public Licence
    version 2 or later, with an XAudio2 output backend added by this port. It is a library of
    its own and can be replaced by another build of the same version; its source and the
    backend are in this port's repository (w10m/openal, w10m/patches/openal-soft).
    It includes {fmt}, (c) Victor Zverovich and contributors, MIT licence.

vcruntime140.dll, msvcp140.dll
    The Microsoft Visual C++ runtime for UWP apps (Microsoft.VCLibs), redistributed under
    Microsoft's terms for it.

gamefiles\
    The files reVC adds to and replaces in the game's own (from the reVC repository). The
    game's data itself is not in this package: the app copies it from the player's own copy
    of Grand Theft Auto: Vice City.
