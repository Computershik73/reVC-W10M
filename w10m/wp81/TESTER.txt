reVC (GTA Vice City) for Windows Phone 8.1 - what to test

WHAT IT IS
The game itself, built for Windows Phone 8.1 for the first time. It needs your own copy of
Grand Theft Auto: Vice City for PC (about 1.5 GB). Nothing of the game's data is in the package.
File: @PACKAGE@ (installs as an app of its own, "reVC")

WHAT YOU NEED
- A phone with Windows Phone 8.1 (NOT Windows 10 Mobile), developer-unlocked or interop-unlocked,
  as for the trial app.
- 1 GB of RAM or more. On a 512 MB phone the app warns that the phone will most likely close
  the game: please try it anyway if that is all you have, and say so.
- About 3.5 GB free: 1.5 GB for the game folder you copy to the phone, and 1.5 GB inside the
  app. The folder you copied can be deleted once the app has its copy.
- The PC game's folder: the one with the folders audio, data, models, anim, txd and TEXT.

STEPS
1. Install the .appx the same way you installed the trial.
2. Connect the phone by USB and copy the whole game folder to it (to Documents or to the SD card).
3. Open reVC. Tap "Choose the game's folder...". The phone's folder picker opens: choose the
   game folder and confirm. The app comes back and copies the game: several minutes. Keep the
   app open and the screen on.
4. When the copy is done, the game starts. Wait for its menu, then start a new game. Walk, get
   into a car, drive for a few minutes.
5. Please also try:
   - the Back button: in the game it should pause / go back in the game's menus, not leave it;
   - turning the phone to the other landscape side;
   - the on-screen controls (left half of the screen: move; right half: camera);
   - leaving with the Start button and coming back: the game should be paused, not restarted;
   - the RES button in the game's menus (picture size; it takes effect at the next start).

WHAT TO SEND BACK
1. The phone: model, RAM, OS version (Settings > About > More info).
2. What worked and what did not: the install; coming back from the folder picker; the copy (how
   long it took); the game's menu; a new game; sound (voices in the first cutscene, the radio in
   a car); the controls; the Back button.
3. How smooth it is walking and driving, and whether the phone gets hot.
4. The log. Two places have a "Send the log" button:
   - the app's first screen (the one with "Choose the game's folder...");
   - in the game: Options > About the Port.
   It opens the phone's share screen: send it by e-mail, a message or OneDrive. If the game
   closes by itself, open the app again and send the log from its first screen right away: it
   carries the run that closed as well.
5. Screenshots (Power + Volume Up) or photos of anything that looks wrong.

IF IT DOES NOT WORK
- The install is refused: send the exact error text.
- The app closes at once: open it again and send the log; if it never gets as far as its first
  screen, say after how many seconds it closes.
- The folder picker comes back and nothing happens: send the log.
